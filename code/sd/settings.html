Installed sensors
The drop must stay above the limit, with water flowing, for this long before the alarm fires, so short pressure spikes are ignored. It clears after the same time back below the limit.

Leak alarm

Water that never stops usually means a dripping tap, a running cistern or a burst pipe.

Normal use stops now and then; a leak doesn't. 0 turns this rule off. Raise it if you water the garden for longer.

Fault limits

Past these limits the unit raises a fault; on a Controller it also stops the pump until the error is cleared.

Keep them outside the pump's start and stop pressures (System Parameters), or the pump trips on every start.
Raise the dry-run time for a pump that takes long to prime.

Pump starts

A pump that starts many times an hour usually means a pressure tank that has lost its air, or a leak. It wears the motor and the relay.

0 turns the alarm off. Starts in manual mode don't count.

Password

With a password, every change (settings, pump, restart, updates, uploads) asks for it. Readings stay open. The user name is admin.
